## Context

Visa is a global leader in digital payments. Since Visa acquired Tink in 2021, the two companies have worked to bring Tink's platform up to Visa's stringent security standards, establishing task forces to drive availability, resilience, and attack-surface improvements across the platform.

## What I did

- Joined one of the task forces as a Security Champion: reviewed CI/CD-flagged findings and worked directly with code authors to fix them, not just file tickets.
- Completed an intensive multi-day security course through Visa University.
- Was given the role by Visa to improve code quality and uptime across Tink's entire product suite, after a period of downtime incidents. Together with the task force I made sure other teams followed high code standards and drained their backlog of flagged findings.

## Outcome

Tink's codebase stability climbed toward Visa's 99.99% monthly uptime target as the volume of unresolved CI/CD-flagged findings drained down.
