## Context

Valtech is a global digital consultancy. Its talent programme brings in developers early and trains them in the way Valtech builds for clients. I chose the MACH track: Microservices, API-first, Cloud-native and Headless architecture.

## What I did

- Followed the MACH track, learning to design systems as independent services behind clean APIs, with the frontend decoupled from the backend.
- Worked full-time at Valtech while studying full-time at Uppsala University.
- Put the training straight into client work on the Radon assignment, where the platform was rebuilt around exactly these patterns.

## Outcome

The programme gave me the architecture habits I still build with: small services with clear contracts, so one part of a system can change without breaking the rest. I left Valtech when the chance came to write my Master's thesis at Tink.
